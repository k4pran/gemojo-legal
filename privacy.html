Gemojo – Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 7, 2026

Introduction

Gemojo ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Gemojo application ("App") handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information.

The App does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal details such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

Data Storage

Data created and used within the App, including images and rendered outputs, is stored locally on your device or in storage locations you choose. We do not upload, transmit, or store your data on our servers or in any cloud service operated by us.

Data Sharing

We do not share your data with third parties.

The App does not send your information to us, to analytics providers, to advertisers, or to any other third party.

Exported Content

The App allows you to export rendered images to your device's local storage. Any export is initiated solely by you and remains under your control. Exported files are saved locally and are not transmitted or shared by the App.

Permissions

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Storage / Media access: Required to load images and save exported renders on your device.

These permissions are used solely for the App's core functionality and not for data collection.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services such as analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or social media SDKs that collect user data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
